                  What Unravel borrows

                  Google Calendar
                  Kept: day and week grids, a color for every block, a look at where the hours went.
                  Left out: invitations and shared calendars.
                  Habitica
                  Kept: dailies, quests, rewards and a character that grows.
                  Left out: health points and damage for a missed day.
                  The Pomodoro Technique
                  Kept: 25-minute rounds, a short break between them, a longer one after four.
                  Left out: rest as a countdown. Recovery counts up.
                  Atomic Habits
                  Kept: votes for an identity, habit stacking, the two-minute minimum, never missing twice.
                  Left out: streak counters that punish a blank day.
                  Carl Jung
                  Kept: a personality made of many selves, archetypes and the shadow.
                  Left out: diagnosis. These are prompts for reflection, not psychology.
                  How to use this reference

                  This is a working component specimen with sample data. Checking, planning and adding affect this page only. Reloading restores the examples; your theme choice is remembered separately.

                  The connected app keeps its routes, navigation and data. Use design.md for the current rules and design-notes.md for the surface decisions.
